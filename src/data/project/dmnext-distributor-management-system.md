## Overview

DMNext represents the next generation of business systems work: designing workflows around the actual operation instead of forcing the operation to fit a generic tool.

## The Problem

Operational information is often scattered across spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and manual handoffs. The goal is to create a single system that makes work easier to execute, control, and improve.

## My Approach

I focus on understanding the process first, then translating it into data models, screens, validations, permissions, and reports that support how people actually work.

## Key Focus Areas

- Centralized operational data
- Clear workflow ownership and status
- Better reporting and traceability
- Maintainable system architecture
- Practical user-focused interfaces

## Lessons Learned

The strongest systems are not simply feature-rich. They make the intended business process easier to understand and execute consistently.
