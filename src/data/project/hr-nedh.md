## Overview

The system centralizes employee information while supporting hospital-specific scheduling and workforce processes.

## Core Areas

- Employee information and compensation
- Attendance
- Duty roster scheduling
- Leave application and approval
- Reports and payroll connectivity

## Approach

The system is designed around flexible scheduling and operational workflows so HR can manage records without forcing hospital operations into a rigid office-hours model.
